# Concepts

The words that mean something specific here, so the rest of the docs can use them without
re-explaining each time.

## Graph

The structure the products read from and write to. Everything else in this documentation eventually
describes something that reads a graph or writes one.

## Product

One of the things LocusGraph ships. A product owns four public surfaces and no more: a marketing
page on `www`, an articles collection on `www`, an app on its own subdomain, and a section of these
docs. Nothing else is a public URL.

## Section

One product's documentation, rooted at `/{product}` on this host. A section owns its own sidebar,
its own reading order, and its own pages.
