# Deployment

Two ways to run LocusGraph. Both are operated by us; they differ in whether the infrastructure underneath is shared.

| | **Managed** | **Dedicated** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who operates it | Us | Us |
| Infrastructure | Shared regions | Yours alone |
| Data location | Our region | A region you choose |
| You operate | Nothing | Nothing |
| Upgrades | Automatic | Scheduled with you |
| Client change | — | `serverUrl` |

## Managed

The default. Point the client at a region server and hold a key.

```typescript
const client = new LocusGraphClient({
  serverUrl: "https://api.locusgraph.com",
  agentSecret: process.env.LOCUSGRAPH_AGENT_SECRET,
});
```

Your graphs are isolated from every other customer's by the [graph boundary](/locusgraph/enterprise/security), which is structural rather than a filter — a search in one graph cannot reach another, at all. For most teams that is the right answer and there is nothing further to decide.

## Dedicated

A LocusGraph instance that is yours alone — your own database and vector index, in a region you choose. We operate it; you do not.

```typescript
const client = new LocusGraphClient({
  serverUrl: "https://acme.locusgraph.com",
  agentSecret: process.env.LOCUSGRAPH_AGENT_SECRET,
});
```

That one line is the whole client-side difference. Every call on every page of these docs works unchanged.

Reach for it when the requirement is **isolation of infrastructure**, not just isolation of data:

- A compliance regime that asks whose workload shares the database
- A region the shared service does not cover
- An upgrade cadence you need a say in
- Capacity that should not contend with anyone else's

<Callout tone="note" title="Dedicated is still fully managed">
  You run nothing. The difference from Managed is that the machinery underneath is not shared, and
  the region is your choice.
</Callout>

## Running it in your own network

Deployments inside a customer's own infrastructure are arranged under agreement rather than self-serve, and are not documented here. If data genuinely may not leave your network, start a conversation — the answer depends on your environment, and on terms rather than a download link.

Worth having ready: the regulation or policy driving the requirement, the environment you would run it in, and whether [deep recall](/locusgraph/recall/deep-recall) and ingest are in scope for your data policy. Those three decide most of it.

## Choosing

1. **Infrastructure must not be shared, or the region is not covered** → Dedicated
2. **Data may not leave your network** → talk to us
3. **Otherwise** → Managed

Most teams stop at three. The other two exist for requirements you already know you have — if you are unsure which applies, you want Managed.

## Moving between them

Graphs do not follow a change of `serverUrl`. Moving from Managed to Dedicated is a data migration, not a configuration change. Plan it as one, and prefer deciding correctly at the start.
